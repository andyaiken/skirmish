Privacy Policy

Skirmish · Last updated 18 September 2026

Skirmish does not collect any information about you. There are no accounts to sign up for, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The game does not send anything to us, and we have no servers that could receive it.

Your campaign

Your campaign and your settings are saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your campaign is also kept in your own iCloud account so that you can carry it on from your other devices. That copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account, and we cannot see it or get to it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Deleting the game removes the copy on that device. To remove the iCloud copy as well, abandon your campaign in the game's options before deleting it, or manage your iCloud storage in your device's settings.

Purchases

Card packs are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and never shares your payment details with us. The game asks the App Store only which packs you own, so that it can unlock them.

Children

Because Skirmish collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before any new version of the game does anything differently, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the Skirmish issues page.
