# Welcome to Skirmish

Skirmish is a turn-based tactical game about leading a team of heroes to conquer an island. The island is divided into regions, and each region is held by monsters. Win the encounters in a region to claim it, then use its rewards to make your team stronger and reach new parts of the island.

## What you are trying to do

1. Build a team of five heroes.
2. Choose a reachable region on the campaign map and fight its encounters.
3. Use rewards, money, equipment, and your stronghold to prepare for the next fight.
4. Keep conquering regions until the island is yours.

Your heroes gain experience by surviving encounters. They can learn new features, find equipment, and recruit allies as the campaign progresses. The battles are tactical: where you move, what action you choose, and how you use each hero's strengths all matter.

## The campaign screens

- **Campaign map**: Choose which region to attack or buy. Each region shows what you may face and the reward for taking it.
- **Stronghold**: Build and manage structures that give your campaign and encounters useful benefits.
- **Heroes**: Recruit, review, equip, and improve your team.
- **Items**: Manage spare equipment and consumables, shop, and enchant items.

Start by creating five heroes. When you are unsure what to do next, select a region on the campaign map and follow the available actions in its details panel. During a battle, select **Help** for the encounter guide.
