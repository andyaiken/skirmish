# Your Heroes

The Heroes page shows the heroes you have recruited. Select a hero to open their character sheet, review their statistics and cards, manage their equipment, or level them up. A reward that recruits a hero appears on this page; select it and complete the hero builder.

You can lead up to ten heroes in a campaign, but you can send no more than five into one encounter. Choose the heroes best suited to the region and its enemies. Heroes who survive an encounter gain XP; heroes who die are lost.

## The character sheet

- **Statistics** shows the hero's traits, skills, damage bonuses and resistances, XP, proficiencies, and auras.
- **Equipment** shows what the hero has equipped or is carrying. You can equip an item only if the hero can use it. Each kind of weapon equipped, and any implement, also gives the hero a standard attack card in encounters.
- **Features** shows the cards that may be offered when the hero levels up.
- **Actions** shows the cards in the hero's deck, which provide actions during encounters. Standard cards, including the ones equipment gives, are not part of the deck and are always in hand.

Some useful statistics:

- **Endurance** helps a hero withstand incoming damage.
- **Resolve** determines how many wounds they can take before becoming unconscious.
- **Speed** determines their movement points at the start of a turn.
- **Reactions** helps determine the order combatants act in.
- **Brawl**, **Spellcasting**, and **Weapon** are skills used by different attacks. Other skills support actions such as Stealth, Perception, and Presence.
- **Auras** affect combatants standing next to the hero, as described by the aura.

## Leveling up

Heroes gain XP when they survive an encounter. When a hero has enough XP, open their character sheet and choose a feature from the cards offered. The feature is added to that hero.

## Rewards

Hero rewards appear on this page. Select a reward card to redeem it. Depending on the card, you may recruit a hero, give a hero XP, or level up a chosen hero.
