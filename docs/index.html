Skirmish

A tactical battle game for iPad and Mac

Lead a band of heroes across an island, one encounter at a time. As you take control of more of the island, your heroes grow stronger, and you pick up allies and magic items to help you in the fights ahead.

Getting help

If something isn't working, or you have a question or a suggestion, please raise it on the Skirmish issues page. It helps to say which device you are playing on and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Card packs

The game is complete without buying anything. Card packs add new species, roles and backgrounds for your heroes, new monsters to face, and new items, potions, scrolls and buildings. Each is bought once through the App Store.

Packs you buy on one device appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. If one is missing, for example after reinstalling the game, open Help (the ? button), go to Options, and choose Restore Purchases.

Playing on more than one device

If you are signed in to iCloud, your campaign follows you between your iPad and your Mac. Play on one, and the other picks up where you left off. It usually takes a few seconds, and returning to the game makes it check again.

If you play on two devices while they are offline, the game will ask which campaign to carry on with when they reconnect, showing when each was last played and how far it has got.
